# Data rights and attribution

This repository contains project-authored metadata, code, documentation and
derived research tables. Source-specific rights continue to apply; inclusion in
this repository does not relicense upstream material.

## IMF PortWatch

Port-call and passage indicators must retain the following attribution:

> Source: International Monetary Fund, PortWatch, https://portwatch.imf.org/.

Reuse remains subject to IMF copyright and usage terms. Neither the IMF nor
PortWatch endorses this study. The shared daily values were selected into fixed
calendar windows and field names were standardized by the authors; this is a
project transformation and is not attributable to the IMF.

## Copernicus and European Commission products

Raw Sentinel imagery, TROPOMI pixel stacks, ERA5 grids and GHS-POP rasters are
not redistributed. Derived values retain source attribution and modification
notices. Official access and citation routes are listed in
`docs/THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md`.

The eligible-date SAR densities and population-weighted daily NO₂ aggregates
are author-produced transformations. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement
by Copernicus data providers or the European Commission.

## xView3

Third-party xView3 code and weights are excluded. Users must obtain them from
the official repository under their original licence. Packaged outputs remain
vessel-like labels and must not be represented as verified vessel identities or
counts.

## Repository-wide reuse

The scoped MIT License in `LICENSE` applies only to project-authored software
code. It does not license the contents of `data/`, manuscript figures,
satellite imagery, model weights or provider-derived values. Source-specific
rights and provider terms continue to govern those materials.
